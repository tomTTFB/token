What you can do here

  • Flash firmware to a connected Token over USB, straight from the browser.
  • Add a 2FA account by scanning a QR code or entering a secret manually.

Listing, deleting, changing the PIN, and wiping accounts are all done on the device itself (Settings menu) — the firmware doesn't expose those over serial yet, so this tool can't drive them remotely.
